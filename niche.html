Sales Rep Playbook & Onboarding Guide

1 Select Your Target Niche & Location

Identify high-intent industries (e.g., Dispensaries in Denver). Physical locations require phone or walk-in strategies, while digital niches thrive on outbound social messages.

2 Match Method to Platform

Always choose the primary platform where decision-makers engage most actively. For instance, use Instagram DMs for Nightlife/Hospitality, but LinkedIn or Phone Calls for Law Firms.

3 Dynamic Custom Entries

If a client operates on a new platform or sub-channel (e.g., Threads, Bluesky), use the + Custom button to dynamically append it to your targeting database on the fly.
